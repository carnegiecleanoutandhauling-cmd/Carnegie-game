How To Haul

Junk keeps coming off the curb — stack it tight in the truck bed. Fill an entire row edge-to-edge and it gets hauled off to the dump.

  • Move pieces: ← → or swipe buttons
  • Rotate: ↑ / tap ⟳
  • Soft drop: ↓
  • Hard drop: Space / tap DROP
  • Pause: P

Let the pile reach the cab and the truck's full — route's over. Every load you clear speeds up the next day on the job.
